# PRIVACY POLICY

## Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to PizzaHub.

PizzaHub supports account, product browsing, cart, checkout, and delivery flows. Order, payment, and delivery workflows are handled within the application and are not connected to external fulfillment or payment providers.

## 1. Application Contact

PizzaHub is maintained by its application owner. Privacy-related questions or requests may be submitted through the contact information provided with the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use PizzaHub, the application may collect information such as your name, email address, authentication-related data managed through Supabase Authentication, checkout details entered by the user, and basic technical or session information required for website functionality and security.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, maintain login sessions, support password reset functionality, display user-related content, support the cart and checkout flow, and improve application reliability and security.

## 4. Application Scope

The current version of PizzaHub supports account, product browsing, cart, checkout, and delivery flows. Order, payment, and delivery workflows are handled within the application experience.

## 5. Checkout and Delivery Data

Information entered during checkout, including delivery address or contact details, is used to support the checkout and delivery workflow within PizzaHub.

## 6. Payment Data

PizzaHub does not process payment transactions and does not store payment card details. Any payment-related interface, if present, is non-transactional in the current version.

## 7. Authentication Services

PizzaHub uses Supabase Authentication to support account creation, login, logout, and password reset functionality. Authentication-related information is handled through Supabase infrastructure.

## 8. Cookies and Session Data

PizzaHub may use essential session technologies required for authentication, navigation, and website functionality. The application currently does not use advertising or marketing tracking cookies.

## 9. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

PizzaHub may rely on third-party services such as Supabase, hosting providers, social media links, and mapping services. These services may process technical or usage-related data according to their own privacy practices.

## 10. International Data Processing

Because third-party infrastructure may operate in different regions, some data may be processed or stored outside the user's country of residence. PizzaHub uses these services only to support the application's technical functionality.

## 11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## 12. Data Retention

User account information and application data are retained only as long as needed to provide application functionality, support the user flow, or maintain the application.

## 13. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your information. Such requests may be submitted through the contact information provided with the application.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application evolves. Updated versions will be published on this page.

## 15. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided with PizzaHub.
