# PRIVACY POLICY

## Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to PizzaHub.

PizzaHub is a demo and portfolio food ordering application built to demonstrate frontend architecture, user authentication, routing, cart behavior, checkout flow, and product browsing. PizzaHub is not a real commercial business and does not process real food orders or real payments.

## 1. Project Owner

For the purposes of this demo project, PizzaHub is maintained by the developer presenting the application as part of a frontend portfolio. Privacy-related questions or requests may be submitted through the contact information provided with the project or portfolio.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use PizzaHub, the application may collect information such as your name, email address, authentication-related data managed through Supabase Authentication, checkout details entered by the user, and basic technical or session information required for website functionality and security.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, maintain login sessions, support password reset functionality, display user-related content, demonstrate the cart and checkout flow, and improve application reliability and security.

## 4. Demo Project Notice

PizzaHub is created for educational and portfolio purposes. Any checkout or ordering flow shown in the application is intended to demonstrate product design and frontend implementation, not to complete a real commercial transaction.

## 5. Checkout and Delivery Data

Information entered during checkout, including delivery address or contact details, is used only to demonstrate how a food ordering workflow could work in a real application. This information is not used for real delivery unless explicitly stated.

## 6. Payment Data

PizzaHub does not process real payments and does not store payment card details. Any payment-related interface, if present, is for demonstration purposes only.

## 7. Authentication Services

PizzaHub uses Supabase Authentication to support account creation, login, logout, and password reset functionality. Authentication-related information is handled through Supabase infrastructure.

## 8. Cookies and Session Data

PizzaHub may use essential session technologies required for authentication, navigation, and website functionality. The application currently does not use advertising or marketing tracking cookies.

## 9. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

PizzaHub may rely on third-party services such as Supabase, hosting providers, social media links, and mapping services. These services may process technical or usage-related data according to their own privacy practices.

## 10. International Data Processing

Because third-party infrastructure may operate in different regions, some data may be processed or stored outside the user's country of residence. PizzaHub uses these services only to support the demo application's technical functionality.

## 11. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. As a portfolio project, PizzaHub is not intended to represent a fully audited production security environment.

## 12. Data Retention

User account information and demo-related data are retained only as long as needed to provide application functionality, demonstrate the user flow, or maintain the project.

## 13. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your information. For this demo project, such requests may be submitted through the contact information provided with the project or portfolio.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application evolves. Updated versions will be published on this page.

## 15. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided with the PizzaHub project or portfolio.
